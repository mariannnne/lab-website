Science Communication

Our lab has created an outreach initiative called Science is Service. We are always looking for collaborations with people from our community who feel connected with our work in some way and want to discuss it. We also collaborate with local artists and comedians to create engaging communications that make understanding advances in science enjoyable. Learn more here: ​https://www.science-is-service.com/
